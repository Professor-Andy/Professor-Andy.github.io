The Wall That Heals is the largest replica of The Vietnam Veterans Memorial in Washington, D.C. in the country. Since 1996, The Wall and its Education Center have traveled the country, giving Vietnam Veterans the welcome home they deserve and families an opportunity to honor their loved ones. We are honored to bring this experience to our community and recognize all those who served in the Vietnam War.
