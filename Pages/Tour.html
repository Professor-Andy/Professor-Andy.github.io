These 1-hour group tours provide a meaningful and insightful experience designed to honor those who served in Vietnam. These tours can be tailored for specific age groups and mobility issues. Tours will help visitors understand the history of the war, provide a space to reflect and remember, honor those who served, and include the Education Center and artifacts.

Perfect For:

  • Schools & Students
  • Veteran Organizations
  • Community Groups
  • Service Groups
